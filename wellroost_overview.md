## From Confusion to Clarity: Data-Driven Decisions

The path to smart property management and home investment is often fragmented. Renovation advice emphasizes aesthetics while ignoring equity recovery and financing carry costs, while personal finance sites skip the physical realities of home systems, utility efficiency, and structural maintenance. The result is costly decision paralysis and budget blowouts.

WellRoost was founded on a simple premise: empowerment through integrated strategy and transparent math.

We built WellRoost to be the resource we wished we had: a decision engine where empirical remodeling, energy optimization, and mortgage/equity calculations replace guesswork.

---

## What “WellRoost” Means

* **Well**: Inspired by wellness, well-being, peak operational efficiency, and sound financial decision-making. It represents optimized living and lower total cost of ownership.
* **Roost**: Your sanctuary, your household anchor, and the foundational equity asset of your family's future. It represents home security, stability, and wealth-building.

Together, WellRoost is about using smart, data-driven frameworks to unlock the full equity potential of your property and your lifestyle.

## Our Audience

1. **The High-Stakes Renovator**: Homeowners evaluating $20k–$150k+ renovations looking to maximize after-repair equity (comp ARV) and avoid contractor over-improvement traps.
2. **The Move vs. Improve Evaluator**: Families weighing the 5-year financial ledger of selling and relocating against expanding their current footprint.
3. **The Energy & Systems Optimizer**: Property owners upgrading heat pumps, solar, insulation, and smart automation while maximizing Section 25C, HEAR, and HOMES rebates.
4. **The Real Estate & Equity Investor**: Investors and landlords underwriting properties with 70% rule Maximum Allowable Offers (MAO), combined LTV (CLTV) thresholds, and ADU rental cash flows.
