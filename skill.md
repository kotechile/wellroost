# Agent Operating Skill: Vibe Marketing & Technical Copywriting (wellroost.com)

## Core Directives
1. **Audience & Tone**: Direct, high-signal, pragmatic, and authoritative. Write for high-stakes property decision-makers, renovators, investors, and homeowners evaluating $20k–$150k+ capital expenditures. Zero fluff. No generic real estate clichés.
   - **Target Profiles**:
     - *The High-Stakes Renovator*: Focused on after-repair equity (comp ARV), contractor over-improvement traps, and cost-to-value yields.
     - *The Move vs. Improve Evaluator*: Analyzing the 5-year financial ledger, friction carry costs, and relocation vs. footprint expansion.
     - *The Energy & Systems Optimizer*: Modeling heat pump payback horizons, solar TCO, and federal/state incentives (Section 25C, HEAR, HOMES).
     - *The Real Estate & Equity Investor*: Underwriting maximum allowable offers (MAO 70% rule), combined LTV (CLTV) thresholds, and ADU rental yields.
2. **Structural Style (Smart Brevity)**:
   - Lead with the ultimate conclusion or bold financial takeaway in the first sentence.
   - Use bold lead-ins for bullet points to enable scanning in under 5 seconds.
   - Strip all marketing adjectives, corporate jargon, and introductory pleasantries.
3. **Execution Ratio**: Maintain 95% AI generation for plumbing, data pipelines, schema generation, and component scaffolding, and 5% human intervention for final strategic positioning, taste validation, and design-system tokens.

## Output Specifications
- **Code**: Clean, modular React/TypeScript components using Tailwind CSS utility classes and Astro islands (`client:visible` / `client:load`). Adhere to WellRoost's design system tokens (zinc surfaces, cyan/emerald accents, deep navy anchors, GT America/Geist typography).
- **Copy**: Crisp, data-backed value propositions paired with an interactive decision engine hook. Replace static download offers with immediate, runnable interactive assets hosted on `wellroost.com`.
