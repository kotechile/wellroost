# WellRoost Vibe Marketing Lexicon

## Preferred Terminology
- **Interactive Asset / Decision Engine**: Replaces "lead magnet", "ebook", "renovation checklist", or "downloadable PDF guide".
- **Net Yield / ROI Engine / Equity Recovery Ledger**: Replaces "calculator" or "renovation estimator" when financial and property equity metrics are involved.
- **Agentic Plumbing / Telemetry Pipeline**: Replaces "backend automation", "zapier zap", or "workflow scripts".
- **Precision Mechanics / Empirical Framework**: Replaces "advanced features", "cool tools", or "renovation tips".
- **Comp ARV / Capital Expenditure (CapEx) Underwriting**: Replaces "home valuation guess" or "renovation cost estimate".
- **Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) / Payback Horizon**: Replaces "energy savings" or "utility discounts".
- **Over-Improvement Trap / Diminishing Returns Threshold**: Replaces "costly remodeling mistakes".
- **Relocation Friction Delta / Carry Cost**: Replaces "moving expenses" or "mortgage hassle".

## Prohibited Terms (Forbidden AI-isms & Fluff)
- Delve, harness, testament, tapestry, landscape, beacon, unleash, supercharge, revolutionize, elevate, embark, plethora, synergy.
- Clichéd housing marketing jargon: "dream home", "sanctuary oasis", "forever home", "nest egg", "game-changer", "curb appeal magic".
- "In today's fast-paced digital world..." (Ban all generic throat-clearing intros).
- "When it comes to home improvement..." (Ban conversational introductory filler).
- "It is important to keep in mind that..." (Ban bureaucratic passive hedges).
